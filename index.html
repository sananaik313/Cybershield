        Four rules to remember

        • Never share your OTP, UPI PIN or card PIN with anyone, even someone who says they are from your bank.
        • Real offices do not threaten you by SMS or ask you to stay on a video call.
        • Do not install an app from a link. A file ending in .apk can read your messages and OTPs.
        • You never need a PIN or QR scan to receive money. Check your own bank balance, not a screenshot.
